Hello,
I’m Craig.

I began in software engineering and later moved through product design and product management. It’s a useful combination: I can ask why we’re building something, consider how people will use it, and worry just enough about whether it will actually work.

Outside work I write about Japanese folklore, learn about cultures and languages, take photographs, wander around the Lake District and acquire hobbies at an alarming rate. Most require books. A few require boots. All seem to require tea.
