Inside the thinking

Good UX goes
beyond the UI.

The tools have changed. Humans still scan, guess, get distracted and try the door marked PULL by pushing it. These are the fundamentals I keep coming back to.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Understand the problem.

Speak to real people. Learn what they are actually trying to do. Resist designing for a perfectly patient imaginary person who reads every instruction.

02 / CRAFT

Make complex feel simple.

Make the next action obvious, show what happened and give people a way back when things go pear-shaped.

03 / THE FUTURE

Build beyond the trend.

New tools arrive every Tuesday. Human attention, patience and common sense are still in fairly limited supply.
