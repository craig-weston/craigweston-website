THE OFTEN-MISSED INVESTMENT

Teams ship features.
But do people succeed?

Roadmaps, releases and redesigns are visible. The hidden cost is often a failed task, repeated support contact, clumsy hand-off or unnecessary step that nobody has stopped to examine.

01 / REVENUE

Stop losing people at the last step.

Help people finish the task: buy, book, apply, set up an account, use a feature or complete an important workflow.

Opportunity: higher task success and conversion
02 / OPERATIONS

Reduce avoidable work.

When customers or staff cannot complete a task, work gets repeated, tickets build up and colleagues step in. Clearer journeys can reduce avoidable effort.

Opportunity: fewer support contacts and escalations
03 / DELIVERY

Reduce rework before the next release.

Find flawed assumptions, low-value steps and confusing interactions before they become costly development or redesign work.

Opportunity: less rework
04 / EXPERIENCE

Make your product work for more people.

Review clarity, accessibility, consistency and recovery from errors, including the needs of people using assistive technology.

Opportunity: broader access and trust

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does an audit mean rebuilding the product?

Not necessarily. The best result might be a simpler workflow, clearer status, improved content or a better hand-off. An audit helps decide whether to refine, redesign or rethink a service before investing in delivery.

Will an audit guarantee more sales or lower costs?

No responsible consultant can promise a conversion uplift before examining your context, implementing changes and measuring results. I identify problems and opportunities; the business outcome depends on the solution and how it is tested.

Does the review include accessibility certification?

No. Standard audits include scoped accessibility checks informed by WCAG 2.2. A full accessibility conformance assessment, user testing with disabled participants and legal advice are separate specialist services.

What do I need to share?

A description of your app, portal, product or service, its users and the tasks you want to improve. A URL, test account, prototype or walkthrough may be useful after agreeing scope. Don't share passwords or personal data in an initial enquiry.

Is this just an automated score?

No. Automated tools can detect some issues, but they cannot establish whether a complex workflow or interaction makes sense. The assessment combines structured human review, context and evidence.

Can you review an internal tool or early product concept?

Yes. Employee systems, customer portals, SaaS workflows and prototypes can be reviewed when the agreed journey and necessary access are available. Early reviews can identify risks before build.

Can you implement the recommendations?

We can discuss implementation and design support as a separate scope. The audit itself remains useful even if your own designers or developers make the changes.
